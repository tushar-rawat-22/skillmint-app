# Code of Conduct

SkillMint's public project spaces are for respectful, useful discussion about the product and its engineering.

Be clear, specific and professional. Disagreement is welcome when it focuses on evidence, trade-offs and the work rather than the person. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate humiliation, doxxing, spam and publication of private user information are not acceptable.

Do not post resumes, account credentials, private support conversations, personal identifiers, or other sensitive material in public issues or pull requests. Use the private support or security routes documented in `SUPPORT.md` and `SECURITY.md` when appropriate.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove material that puts users at risk or prevents productive collaboration. Repeated or severe violations may result in participation restrictions.

If conduct itself needs to be reported privately, use the support contact in `SUPPORT.md` and include only the information needed to understand the incident.
